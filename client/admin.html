Homepage Promotions

Slots 1 and 2 sit side by side on desktop: slot 1 is the wide banner on the left, slot 2 the narrow one on the right. Upload slot 1 at 1940×600 and slot 2 at 824×600. When either is empty the homepage falls back to random product images.

Slot 3 is the strip above them. Scrolling announcements sit on the left and cannot be tapped; icon tiles sit on the right and stay still. Tile icons upload at 120×120. Set a background colour on any announcement to recolour the whole strip.

Slot 4 tiles pin beside a category's product row and alternate left and right down the page. Upload at 600×1300. Tiles fill the slot edge to edge, so keep logos and text inside the middle 70% vertically — the row height follows the products next to it, and the edges crop. Video is allowed here only: 30 seconds and 30MB maximum, .mp4, .mov or .webm. A video plays right through; a still holds for 5 seconds before the next tile.

Slots 5 and 6 are curated homepage category grids, each tile pinned to a category and labelled with its title. Slot 5 renders as a row of small square tiles (upload square, e.g. 400×400) — good for browsing sub-categories at a glance. Slot 6 renders as a row of larger cards with the label overlaid at the bottom (upload at 800×1000) — good for a handful of broad departments. Neither needs a link URL: tapping a tile goes straight to its pinned category.
