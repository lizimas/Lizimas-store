Getting a faster answer

When you message us about an existing order, include your order number and a short description of the problem. If an item arrived damaged or incorrect, a photo helps us resolve it in one exchange rather than several.

Keeping your account safe

Lizimas Store will never ask you for your password, your mobile money PIN, or a one-time authentication code — not in chat, not on WhatsApp, not by phone.

If someone claiming to represent Lizimas Store asks for any of these, do not provide them, and report it to us on the channels above.
