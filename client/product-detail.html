    Pickup Station

    Delivery time starts from the day you place your order until your package arrives at your selected pickup station. You will be notified as soon as your order is ready for collection.

    Please collect your order within 5 days of its arrival. Orders not collected within this period will be automatically cancelled.

    Note: You can pay with cash at the pickup station, or with MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank card or Stanbic Flexipay on the number given to you. Make sure you are given a receipt after paying.

    Door Delivery

    Delivery time is counted from the day you place your order until the day our rider first comes to deliver it to you.

    We deliver between 8:00am and 6:00pm.

    • Within Kampala — usually same day or next day.
    • Outside Kampala — typically 2 to 3 days, depending on the route.
    • Shipped items — some products are brought in specially and take 10 to 14 days. We confirm the timeline with you after you order.

    Our rider calls you on the phone number you gave at checkout before arriving, so please keep it reachable. If nobody is available, we will try again. Repeated failed attempts may mean the order is returned to the store and cancelled.

    The delivery fee is shown at checkout before you confirm. Delivery & tracking help
