# Package Size / Delivery Tier Guide

Package size is based on **how the product normally ships**, not its exact weight or dimensions.

Choose the smallest tier that accurately describes the vehicle and handling normally required for delivery.

| Tier | How it ships | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Small | Boda, in the rider's bag | Phones, clothes, cosmetics, small electronics |
| Medium | Boda, strapped or carried on the seat | Microwave, blender, 25kg rice |
| Large | Special hire or small van, one person | TV, mattress, office chair |
| Extra Large | Pickup or truck, two people | Freezers, cookers, sofa sets |

## Quick decision rule

**Small** — Fits safely in a boda rider's bag.

**Medium** — Can still go by boda, but must be strapped down or carried on the seat.

**Large** — Requires a special hire or small van, but one person can normally handle the item.

**Extra Large** — Requires a pickup/truck or normally needs two people to handle safely.

## Important

Do not choose a tier based only on the product's price.

When unsure, choose based on the **normal delivery method and handling requirement**.

Examples:

- Smartphone → Small
- Shirt → Small
- Perfume/cosmetics → Small
- Blender → Medium
- Microwave → Medium
- 25kg bag of rice → Medium
- Large television → Large
- Mattress → Large
- Office chair → Large
- Freezer → Extra Large
- Cooker → Extra Large
- Sofa set → Extra Large
